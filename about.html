OWNERS & HEAD LATIN COACHES

Anton & Polina Skaskiv

Licensed International Judges

Professional Latin dancers and coaches with more than 20 years of teaching experience, achieving outstanding results at the world’s most prestigious competitions.

Career Highlights
  • Ukrainian Champions
  • USDC Finalists
  • Top 20 at the Blackpool Dance Festival
  • Top 14 at the WDC World Championship
  • Winners of the Dutch Open in Assen
  • Winners of the WDC World Championship in Paris – Professional Rising Star Latin
  • Finalists at the UK and International Championships in England – Professional Rising Star Latin
  • Multiple finalists and winners at prestigious international competitions

Former owners of Respect Dance Hall, one of the largest dance studios in Ukraine, based in Kharkiv.

HEAD BALLROOM COACHES

Oleksandr Kalenyuk & Olena Ablitsova

Licensed International Judges

Professional Ballroom dancers and coaches with extensive experience teaching children and adults of all levels, achieving outstanding results at the world’s most prestigious competitions.

Career Highlights
  • USA National & Ukrainian Champions
  • WDC Under 19 World Champions
  • UK Open & International Championship Winners
  • Blackpool Dance Festival Professional Top 12
  • Blackpool Dance Festival Vice-Champions
  • Winners of major US competitions, including Ohio Star Ball, Embassy, Millennium, Manhattan, Empire, and NYDF

Their students have become National and International Champions and Finalists at major competitions across the USA, Europe, and the World, including Blackpool, UK Open, and International Championships.
