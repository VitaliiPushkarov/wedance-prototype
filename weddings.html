How early should we start?

Tell us your wedding date and the type of dance you have in mind. We’ll help plan a realistic preparation timeline.

We don’t have a song yet. Can you help?

Yes. Bring a few favorites or a feeling you’d like to create, and discuss the options with your instructor.

How many lessons will we need?

That depends on your experience, choreography and goals. The studio will confirm a suitable package and price with you.
