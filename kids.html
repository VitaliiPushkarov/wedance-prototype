A few things
to know.

What age can my child start?

Share your child’s age with the studio. The team will confirm available groups and the most suitable starting point.

Do they need any experience?

Beginners are welcome. We’ll discuss their experience and help find a comfortable introduction.

Are there opportunities to progress?

Yes. Talk to our coaches about your child’s interests and potential next steps. Specific groups and schedules are being confirmed.
