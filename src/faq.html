A few things to know

New to this?
You’re not alone.

Let’s make that first visit feel a little easier.

Do I need any dance experience?

Not at all. We welcome complete beginners and experienced dancers. Your instructor will help you start at a comfortable level.

Do I need to bring a partner?

No partner is needed. You’re welcome to come solo or as a couple, and we’ll help you find the right lesson format.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in and shoes with clean soles. Bring a water bottle, and we’ll take care of the first steps.

How do I choose the right program?

Tell us who the lessons are for and what you’d like to try. We’ll talk through private lessons, group classes, or a program built around your goals.
